Danganronpa V3: Killing Harmony Glossary
This reference dictionary outlines the standard fan and official localization terms for Danganronpa V3: Killing Harmony. It is designed for use in translation, consistency checking, and content creation related to the Spanish-speaking community.
Core Concepts and Settings
Ultimate: Definitivo/a (Refers to the students' specialized talents).
Ultimate Academy for Gifted Juveniles: Academia de Definitivos para Jóvenes Talentosos.
Killing Game: Juego de Matanza.
Mastermind: Mente Maestra.
Hope: Esperanza.
Despair: Desesperación.
Monokuma File: Archivo Monokuma.
Body Discovery Announcement: Anuncio de hallazgo del cadáver.
Investigation: Investigación.
Monopad: Monopad.
Gopher Project: Proyecto Gopher.
Exisal: Exisal.
Class Trial Mechanics
Class Trial: Juicio Escolar.
Truth Bullet: Bala de la Verdad.
Lie Bullet / Perjury: Bala de Mentira / Perjurio.
Non-stop Debate: Debate Continuo / Debate Ininterrumpido.
Mass Panic Debate: Debate de Pánico Masivo.
Scrum Debate: Debate Scrum.
Rebuttal Showdown: Enfrentamiento de Refutación.
Argument Armament: Armamento de Argumentos.
Logic Dive: Inmersión Lógica.
Psyche Taxi: Taxi Mental.
Mind Mine: Mina Mental.
Hangman's Gambit: Gambito del Ahorcado.
Closing Argument: Argumento de Cierre.
Spotlight Selection: Selección de Punto de Mira.
Consent: Consentimiento (Used when agreeing with a statement during a debate).
Counter: Contradecir (Used when breaking a statement with a Truth Bullet).
V Point: Punto V (Critical hit on a statement).
Trial Outcomes
Culprit: Culpable.
Blackened: Ennegrecido.
Execution: Ejecución.
Punishment Time: Hora del Castigo.
The Spotless: Los Inocentes (Students who are not the blackened).
